Large datasets are typically the domain of quantitative scholars, who use computational tools to derive numerical insights. More recently, researchers have asked how computational tools and AI might support qualitative scholars in developing rich, interpretive understandings of data.

This project investigates the opportunities and challenges of human-AI collaboration in qualitative analysis. We examine how tools fit into existing analytic practices, the role of time and perspective in interpretive work, and how to design systems that support serendipity and meaning-making rather than reducing qualitative analysis to automation.
