CHI 2022 Workshop on Researcher Wellbeing

Researcher Wellbeing and Best Practices in Emotionally Demanding Research HCI researchers operate in emotionally demanding contexts, including death, illness, online harassment, and structural oppression and violence. In this workshop, we will bring together a group of HCI researchers to:

  • discuss self-care principles and strategies related to mental, physical, and emotional wellbeing for conducting this type of work
  • develop a series of best practices, guidelines, and resources for researcher wellbeing that can be distributed to the broader HCI community

For more information about the CHI 2022 Workshop on Research Wellbeing, please visit the workshop website.
